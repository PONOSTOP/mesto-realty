# Demo assets

Illustrative commercial property photography from Unsplash, downloaded as local WebP files: office towers, workplaces, retail, logistics, production and development land.
Photo identifiers and original URLs are recorded in `scripts/download-assets.js`.
See [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Photos illustrate fictional demo listings, not their exact addresses.
